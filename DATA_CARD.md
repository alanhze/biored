# Data Card: BioRED relation extraction benchmark

This benchmark evaluates document-level biomedical relation extraction on PubMed abstracts annotated in the BioRED format. It uses the original BioRED corpus for training and development, and the 400-abstract BioCreative VIII (BC8) BioRED Subtask 1 test set for the final evaluation.

Raw files live only in `data/` (git-ignored) and in a private Kaggle dataset. This repository publishes IDs, offsets, labels and metrics only.

## 2. Splits

| Split | Source file(s) | Kaggle file(s) | Docs | Mentions | Relations | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | `BioRED/Train.PubTator` + `BioRED/Dev.PubTator` | `biored_train.pubtator` + `biored_dev_orig.pubtator` | 400 + 100 = 500 | 16,884 | 5,340 | relations present |
| dev | `BioRED/Test.PubTator` | `biored_test_orig.pubtator` | 100 | 3,535 | 1,163 | relations present |
| test | `bioredirect/bioredirect_bc8_test.pubtator` (from datasets.zip) | `bc8_test.pubtator` | 400 | 15,404 | 6,036 | ~15,400 / ~6,034 |

**Why the test split comes from BioREDirect.** No file in the official BC8 releases holds gold relations for the 400 test abstracts. `bc8_biored_task1_test.xml` and `.pubtator` contain all 10,000 candidate documents, which hide the 400 scored ones, with mentions but no relations. NCBI's BioREDirect release includes `bioredirect_bc8_test.pubtator`, which has 400 documents with gold relations, and its counts match the BC8 corpus paper. The BioRED team has been asked to confirm that this file matches the official gold.

**Differences from expected counts.** Test relations are 6,036 against the ~6,034 reported in the BC8 corpus paper, and mentions are 15,404 against ~15,400. Both are within rounding or small revisions of the released data.

**Cross-checks that passed.**
- The PubTator and BioC XML versions of each original BioRED file agree on docs, mentions and relations.
- `bc8_biored_task1_train.xml` (500 docs, 5,340 relations) equals original Train + Dev, so the BC8 training release is the same data as this benchmark's train split.

Counts come from `scripts/inventory.py`; test-set details come from `scripts/check_bc8.py`.

### Distribution shift between train and test

The test set is not distributed like the training set, which matters when reading per-class metrics:

- **Drug_Interaction** appears in train (11) and dev (2) but never in test.
- **Cotreatment** and **Conversion** are far more common in test: 172 and 13 relations, against 41 and 3 in a train split of similar size.
- **Entity pairs shift.** ChemicalEntity–GeneOrGeneProduct rises from 14.5% of train relations to 18.5% of test, DiseaseOrPhenotypicFeature–SequenceVariant from 13.5% to 16.2%, and ChemicalEntity–SequenceVariant from 1.1% to 3.3%. ChemicalEntity–DiseaseOrPhenotypicFeature falls from 19.6% to 12.9%.
- **Novelty shifts.** Test has a lower share of Novel relations (61.0%) than train (68.8%) or dev (73.9%).

## 4. Time range

The walkthrough states that the BC8 test abstracts were all published Sep 2022 – Mar 2023, after all training abstracts. **That does not hold for this file.** Most test abstracts are recent, but a large minority are older.

PMIDs are assigned in roughly increasing order, so they work as an approximate date. PMID 36,000,000 was reached around Aug–Sep 2022. From `scripts/split_checks.py`:

| Split | Docs | Min PMID | Median PMID | Max PMID | PMIDs below 36,000,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 500 | 1,353,340 | 19,108,278 | 30,836,660 | 500 |
| dev | 100 | 1,848,636 | 19,207,031 | 28,846,666 | 100 |
| test | 400 | 19,016,528 | 36,212,142 | 36,798,132 | 148 |

- **252 of 400** test abstracts (63%) have PMIDs of 36,000,000 or higher, consistent with publication from about Sep 2022 to early 2023.
- **148 of 400** (37%) have older PMIDs. The oldest, 19016528, is from around 2008.
- **386 of 400** test PMIDs are newer than every train and dev PMID (max 30,836,660, around 2018–2019). The other **14** fall inside the training period.
- **No PMID appears in more than one split**, so there is no document leakage between train, dev and test.

These dates are approximate. Exact publication dates can be taken from PubMed metadata (PMIDs and dates only, no abstract text).

## 5. Known quirks

| Quirk | Where | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relation direction is stored as separate 4-field lines, `PMID id1 id2 Subject:<id>`, not as a column on relation lines | all BioREDirect files | 5,021 in test | `inventory.py` |
| Duplicate mention spans (same PMID, start, end) | test | 2 (15,404 lines, 15,402 unique spans) | `check_bc8.py` |
| Mentions linked to more than one identifier | test | 1,280 | `check_bc8.py` |
| `Organism` used as an entity type instead of `OrganismTaxon` | test | 4 mentions | `check_bc8.py` |
| Relation argument ID with no matching mention in the document: PMID 36106887 (`C001205`–`9606`, Negative_Correlation) and PMID 36290844 (`D000077716`–`79443` and `D000077156`–`79443`, both Positive_Correlation) | test | 3 relations | `label_stats.py` |
| Relation with an OrganismTaxon argument, a pair outside BioRED's usual entity-pair set: PMID 34000358, `59272` (GeneOrGeneProduct, the ACE2 gene ID) Bind `2697049` (OrganismTaxon, the SARS-CoV-2 taxonomy ID) | test | 1 relation | `label_stats.py` |
| BioREDirect train has one relation fewer than the original `Train.PubTator` (4,177 vs 4,178) | BioREDirect train | 1 | `inventory.py` |
| BC8 train XML has fewer mentions than original Train + Dev, though relations match | `bc8_biored_task1_train.xml` | 51 (16,833 vs 16,884) | `inventory.py` |
| Variant identifiers come in several formats: dbSNP (`rs6581593`), HGVS-like (`p\|SUB\|I\|61\|Q`, `c\|INDEL\|5571_5576\|CTAGAT`, `m\|DEL\|3274_3275\|AC`, `g\|SUB\|G\|28881\|A`) and OMIM for some diseases (`OMIM:215700`) | all splits | n/a | direction lines in test |

**Implications for the Phase 2 loader.**
- Treat a line as a relation only if it has 5 or more fields. A rule like "second field is not a number" would read the MeSH-ID direction lines as relations.
- Split identifier fields on `,` and `;` only. Variant IDs contain `|`.
- Normalize `Organism` to `OrganismTaxon`.
- Decide how to score the 4 test relations whose arguments have no mention or an OrganismTaxon type. A model that only pairs annotated Gene, Chemical, Disease and Variant mentions cannot predict them, so they slightly cap recall. Keep them in the gold set so scores stay comparable with the BC8 track, and note them when reporting results.

## 7. Contamination

- All abstracts are public PubMed records, so frontier language models may have read them during pretraining.
- The gold relations are public too: the original BioRED files since 2022 and the BC8 test gold through the BioREDirect release (server date 2025-01-22). Models trained after these releases may have seen the labels themselves, not just the text.
- Test-set scores for pretrained models should therefore be read as an upper bound on performance on unseen literature.
- Contamination risk is higher for the 148 test abstracts with PMIDs below 36,000,000 (section 4), which were in PubMed years before the BC8 track. Reporting scores separately for the older and recent subsets can show whether pretraining exposure inflates results.
